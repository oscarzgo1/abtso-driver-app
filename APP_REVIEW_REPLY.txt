PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Tachyo is a free driver app for employees of haulage and logistics companies that use the Tachyo fleet platform. Drivers clock in and out inside a depot geofence, choose their tractor and trailer, complete walk-around checks, receive and complete loads from their dispatcher with timestamped GPS-tagged photos or an on-screen recipient signature, log fuel and parking, request holiday and see hours and pay. The employer's office sees the same data in a web dashboard. The app is used by many unrelated companies, not one organisation. There are no in-app purchases or paid features.

HOW TO USE THE MAIN FEATURES
1. Sign in with the demo account above. Allow location (While Using, then Always when asked).
2. Clock in, then pick a vehicle and trailer.
3. Complete the walk-around check (you can add a defect with a photo).
4. Open the assigned demo load, accept it, enter the odometer, add a proof-of-delivery photo or signature, and finish it.
5. History tab: hours and shifts. Request a holiday. Weekly Rota lets the driver submit their working days.
6. Clock out, then sign out.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Supabase (database, authentication, file storage, server functions); OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap (map tiles, no account); Apple Core Location via the Tracelet plugin (geofenced clock-in, route during an active shift, photo geotagging); on-device local notifications. No payment processors, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or AI services. No tracking.

BACKGROUND LOCATION
Used only while the driver is clocked in on an active shift, so the employer's dispatch can see position and confirm deliveries. It stops at clock-out, and the iOS location indicator shows while it runs. It is not used for advertising and is not sold.

ACCOUNTS AND DELETION
Employers create and manage driver accounts; there is no self-registration. Account deletion: "Request Account Deletion" on the login screen (signed out, using company code and Driver ID) or in Settings > Account (signed in). The request appears in the employer's dashboard; when the employer confirms it, the driver's account and all attached data (shifts, locations, checks, reports, receipts, holidays, rota and sign-in) are permanently deleted. Email: support@tachyo.co.uk.

USER CONTENT
No public or social content and no user-to-user messaging. Photos, signatures and reports are private to the driver's employer, so content reporting and blocking do not apply.

REGIONS
Identical in all regions. Currency, dates and rules follow the employer's configuration. Mainly used in the UK.
